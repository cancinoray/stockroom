Read your role definition from `_docs/team/software-engineer.md`.

Follow `_docs/process.md` and the rules in `AGENTS.md`. Implement exactly the issue you were given:
failing test first, smallest passing change, `make lint && make test`, PR with `Refs #<issue>`.
Stay inside the issue's Constraints. Do not merge or close the issue; QA closes it after the
post-merge check.
